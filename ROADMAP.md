# duck roadmap

Where the build stands and in what order it runs. The design is in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).

Updated 2026-11-20 17:41Z.

## How the build runs

- The judge is built first: a way to see that a part works before the part exists.
- Up to three lanes at once, where their code does not overlap. A part is done when its end-to-end tests pass on a running coworker.

## Order

1. **Judge.** The replay harness with its scorecard, the end-to-end list from SPEC.md as failing tests, and the `verify` skill.
2. **Core on the test coworker,** in the made-up firm under `examples/`. In this order:
   - A person writes and the coworker answers: setup and install, the model relay, the run, the inbox and the plain `http` door.
   - The proactive cycle: the gate, plug events, the list, the outbox and the sorter in observe.
   - Timers, jobs and checks, notes and dreaming, the archive's tools.
   - The Buzz and text doors.
3. **The first real coworker moves,** with the sorter in observe. The old coworker's sessions are loaded into its archive, and it dreams over them before its first day. The switch happens on the owner's word, with no shadow run before it.
4. **Sorter on,** once its log shows what it would have dropped.
5. **Stage two: a firm that is not duck's makers.** The first real coworker moved on 2026-11-18. Stage two is done when the spec's two tests pass: setup from the repo alone, and joining from one message. In this order:
   - The clean baseline from the fresh-eyes review of 2026-11-18.
   - The join path: duck's own database and keys, its Buzz identity, the member list and duck's DMs; an admin makes a coworker, its person claims it, and `duck install` changes only what differs. The plain path first, the plugin's screens over it second.
   - Settings, the job limit, and the answer to an ask read by duck (rule 13).
   - The office plug with the firm's own app as its first route, and the link that connects a person's mail.
   - History as a stock step, in duck's shape, loaded before the claim.
   - SETUP.md and the setup-from-the-repo test.
   - Team coworkers, and the email door.
   - `duck update`, nightly copies on the server, and a person leaving.
