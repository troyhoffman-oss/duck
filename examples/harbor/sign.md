# How we work at harbor

harbor manages forty apartment buildings for their owners.

- Tenants come first. A tenant who wrote gets an answer within a working day.
- Anything that goes to a tenant or an owner is checked before it leaves: names, units, amounts and dates.
- Rent figures come from the rent roll, never from memory.
- We write plainly and briefly, and sign with our own names.
- Leases, notices and anything with a signature on it are drafted for a person to send. Nobody sends them alone.
